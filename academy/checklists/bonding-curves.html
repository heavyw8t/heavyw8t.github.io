Bonding curve launchpads

Launchpads sell a new token on a bonding curve, then graduate it into a DEX pool once the curve sells out. The bonding curves lesson explains the mechanics. This list covers EVM launchpads.

These are the things to check on every bonding curve launchpad audit, and to get right when you write a launchpad. Go through the every audit list as well.

Tick an item once you have checked it, or mark it N/A when it does not apply. Open an item to see what goes wrong, what to look at, and one way to fix it. The fixes are suggestions: whether one fits depends on the protocol's architecture, design choices and intent.
