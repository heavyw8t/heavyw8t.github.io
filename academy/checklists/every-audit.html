Every audit

Checks that apply to any codebase, whatever the protocol does. This list covers EVM code.

These are the things to check on every audit, and to get right in any contract you write. Go through this list first, then the list for the protocol type.

Tick an item once you have checked it, or mark it N/A when it does not apply. Open an item to see what goes wrong, what to look at, and one way to fix it. The fixes are suggestions: whether one fits depends on the protocol's architecture, design choices and intent.

Every item is done. Untick "Hide done" to see them again.
