Checklists to go through during an audit: one for every codebase, and one for each protocol type.

Checklists

Every audit

Access control, funds in and out, math, reentrancy, signatures, denial of service and upgrades. 25 checks for any EVM codebase.

Bonding curve launchpads

Graduation, trading on the curve, launch setup, and funds and fees. 25 checks for EVM launchpads.

Lending protocols

Liquidation, interest and shares, health checks, oracles, bad debt and configuration. 25 checks for EVM lending markets.
